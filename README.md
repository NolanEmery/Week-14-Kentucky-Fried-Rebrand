# Week-14-Kentucky-Fried-Rebrand
Plan It:

What is the goal of this website?
Make a website that advertises a new look for KFC with emphasis on grilled and air-fried food (to make it look healthy). 

Who are you building for, and what do they care about?
The audience is sceptics of KFC. They need to be convinced to go to KFC.

What is the one big idea New KFC should communicate?
KFC is modern.

Describe how users should experience the website from the moment they visit. What should they see first, and what should happen next?
Include a vertical menu (it must be scrollable and have pictures of the food). In the menu, include the meals "The Lighter Original Bowl" (a chicken salad), "Air-Fried Tender Basket" (chicken), "Colonel's Garden Wrap" (chicken, lettuce, tomato wrap), "Herb & Spice Salad Plate" (chicken strips with salad), "The Smart Bucket" (chicken bucket). 

How should the five menu items be displayed on the page?
In a vertical menu that is scrollable.

What words best describe the look and feel of the rebrand?
Include a modern background (such as white marble) to make it look clean. 

Test It:
No changes necessary.

Expand It:

How did you rework the site into a scrollytelling journey through KFC's history?
Introduce a story with five eras: 1952: The Roadside Kitchen, 1960s-70s: The Franchise Boom, 1990s-2000s: The Bucket Culture, The Slump, and Today: New KFC. Make the story scrollable. Include blocks of text and images on the side for each era. Each era has a photo in the background. Put a button on the bottom right of each era to skip each era.
